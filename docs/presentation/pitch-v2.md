## Delivery notes

Open warmly, as though describing a colleague’s afternoon. Pause after “Why this change?” Look up before revealing Lyceum. Build enthusiasm through visible actions: invite the quotation challenge, let the rejection register, then show the useful export.

Say slower: “The interval does not fix selection bias.” Also slow down for “The student-simulation benchmark has not run.” Look at the judges for both statements and for the ask. Stop talking while an export opens. Avoid narrating every click.

If asked “Is this just AI slop?”, answer: “Judge the artifact with me. Open its source, inspect the exact quotation, check the denominator and edit the output. The deterministic core needs no AI key. A matching quotation can still be misinterpreted, so the lecturer retains responsibility. The simulation remains unvalidated.” Demonstrate the source rather than arguing about AI.

Preflight downloads and prepared examples. If collection fails, disclose the dated sample; if the app fails, narrate the existing backup recording and identify it as recorded. Do not create the impression of a live run. Keep synthetic screens labelled Research sandbox in presentation material; this document does not implement navigation changes.

## One-page judge handout outline

- **Top:** Aisyah’s fictional afternoon, the fragmented evidence problem, and a small source-to-brief diagram.

Include a real quotation with source locator, a denominator and an editable artifact reference, copied from the demonstrated run rather than invented here.
- **Evidence boundary:** Working local prototype, deterministic core, browser-local records. Prepared examples identified. Research sandbox synthetic. Student benchmark not run. Shared accounts and integrations planned.
- **Market strip:** TAM, SAM and SOM from the register, labelled estimates or targets. Lecturer is user; department, faculty or teaching unit is buyer. Skillabi, JobsEQ and curriculum suites provide adjacent capabilities; differentiation is the confirmed evidence chain, not an invented category.
- **Research strip:** Interview lecturers and coordinators across Asian institutions; observe time, handoffs and missing evidence. Test willingness to pay and procurement. Course intelligence comes from identified boards and reviewed samples, not whole-market surveillance.
- **Bottom:** Benchmark design, pilot measures, travel fellowship ask, presenter contact and source links. No outcome promise.
