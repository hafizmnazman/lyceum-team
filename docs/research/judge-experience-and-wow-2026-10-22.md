## 1. Guided tours and self-guided demo modes

- driver.js: MIT, about 5 kB, no dependencies, tours, hints, hooks, React support (https://driverjs.com/) **V**. Shepherd.js is AGPL-3.0 or commercial from version 14; Intro.js is also AGPL or commercial; react-joyride is MIT (3.1.0, React 16.8 to 19).
- Navattic's 2026 report on 40,000 demos (**V**, https://www.navattic.com/report/state-of-the-interactive-product-demo-2026): the top 1% of demos hold viewers 2.2 minutes; 5 to 13 steps per flow; a first step of 10 to 20 words; completion 57% for multi-flow demos against 35%; ungated beats gated. Arcade's benchmarks (16 May 2025, **V**): the top 1% use nine steps; an opening question "which use case?" lifts the play rate 72%.
- Kiosk: Chrome's kiosk flag has no idle reset; add an idle-reset extension and a restricted OS account (https://github.com/substantial/kiosk-manager-chrome).
- A character guiding a tour: Duo welcomes, then asks for a goal before sign-up (https://www.reallygoodux.io/blog/duolingo-user-onboarding); Notion's corner figure asks setup questions. Clippy failed on mistimed interruptions and on being "optimised for first use" (https://www2.cio.co.nz/article/662045/what-went-wrong-clippy-virtual-assistant-pioneer-people-loved-hate/).

## 2. What wins education and AI hackathon demos

- A judge's notes (Magomed Kurbaitaev, 27 July, year not shown): a live working demo beats a more ambitious project on slides "almost every time"; say what it is in the first sentence; a named weakness raised scores; keep a 60-second screen capture as insurance; "pick the sentence you want a judge to repeat to another judge an hour later". https://dev.to/kurbaitaev/what-judges-actually-score-notes-from-a-year-of-hackathon-judging-3p4l
- Wharton Hack-AI-thon, 29 May 2025: the winner (handwriting flags dyslexia, lesson plans drafted) was praised for the clear explanation of its technical reasoning. https://ai-analytics.wharton.upenn.edu/news/fighting-dyslexia-breaking-chatbots-inside-the-winning-projects-of-whartons-2025-hack-ai-thon/
- Imagine Cup: finals are live before a judge panel; semifinals allow a live or pre-recorded demo with live narration. https://imaginecup.microsoft.com/en-us/competition/17897. Google Solution Challenge 2024: the top ten presented live on a demo day. Devpost events cap demo videos at two minutes; one education hackathon judges "a believable before-and-after" (search snippet only, unverified).

## 3. Booth design for a judged ten minutes

- A packing list (EdWeek MarketBrief, 7 May 2015): a sign readable across the room, a monitor on a stand so visitors do not bend over a laptop, a phone hotspot because expo wifi is overloaded; a one-person booth looks harder to approach. https://marketbrief.edweek.org/the-startup-blog/demoing_your_startup_at_an_expo_the_ultimate_packing_list/
- A drop-in booth pattern (Wikimedia, edited March 2026): one short demo ready, non-public internet, leaflets to take away. https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Learning_patterns/Drop-in_demo_booth
- Judges in sequence (UCI showcase tips, 2025): judges rotate independently, 5 to 15 minutes each, let them physically engage, bring multiple units. https://bbcomp.tech.uci.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/BB-Comp-Showcase-Tips-and-Tricks-2025.pdf. A science-fair tip sheet: prepare a 1 to 2 minute and a 5 to 7 minute version. https://stem.idaho.gov/wp-content/uploads/IDSEF/ISEF-Science-Fair-Competition-Tips.pdf
- A memorable booth: TAR UMT's Gold Best Booth at PAASWE, 8 to 12 October 2025; judges noticed a practical bench component. https://eduadvisor.my/articles/tar-umt-paaswe-2025

## 4. Honest wow mechanics

- Count-up counters: only vendor guidance (trigger once, real figures). Precedent: Shopify's Live Globe shows orders per minute (2024, https://www.shopify.com/news/live-globe-2024).
- Live links as receipts: an AAAI 2025 study found citations raised trust even when random, but users who checked them trusted less, so the link must survive checking. https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01303
- Before and after slider: Knight Lab JuxtaposeJS, 24 September 2014, built so journalists "can actually show change". https://knightlab.northwestern.edu/2014/09/24/introducing-juxtaposejs-an-easy-way-to-compare-two-frames/index.html
- A one-click artefact: Microsoft's Copilot event of 16 March 2023 turned a Word file into a deck; the press caveat: "the demos looked wonderful, but how things work out when exposed to real users is an entirely different manner". https://office-watch.com/2023/sneak-peek-at-microsoft-copilot/
- A side-by-side assistant: the MCP launch, 25 November 2024, shipped local servers in Claude Desktop. https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-context-protocol
- A printed receipt: a GitHub-issue receipt printer (The Register, 29 March 2022, https://www.theregister.com/2022/03/29/github_issue_printer/); BERG's Little Printer: paper is "a screen that never turns off".
- A term recap: Wrapped works on self-knowledge, self-expression and comparison. https://adma.com.au/resources/personal-shareable-no-strings-attached-the-winning-formula-behind-spotify-wrapped
- A programme view: small multiples, "the best design solution" for a wide range of problems (Tufte, via Pew, 20 December 2018). https://www.pewresearch.org/decoded/2018/12/20/how-pew-research-center-uses-small-multiple-charts/
- Peak-end: Kahneman et al. 1993; Redelmeier, Katz and Kahneman 2003; Kemp 2008 calls it "not an outstandingly good predictor". Picture superiority: Standing 1973, recognition of 10,000 pictures "almost limitless" (https://www.sas.upenn.edu/psych/rust-lab/publications/standing_73.pdf); Medina's 10% against 65% figure has no primary citation (unverified).

## 5. Mascot presence for judges

- GitHub's brand rules (read 22 October 2026): mascots fit community, celebrations and education "as long as they aren't doing the teaching"; "Don't use mascots to explain, interrupt, or sell"; apologies and crises need proper copy. https://brand.github.com/graphic-elements/mascots
- Mailchimp: Freddie "smiles, winks, and sometimes high-fives, but he does not talk"; "If you're unsure, keep a straight face". https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone/
- Duolingo: Duo "mostly stands still, and doesn't talk or make sounds", reserved for excitement and special occasions (the design page now redirects; unverified).
- Atlassian: the wink only "once you've built trust"; "Delight means little flourishes, not humor"; "Once may amuse, but a dozen times may annoy". https://atlassian.design/foundations/content/voice-tone. Google: never humorous error messages. https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/error-messages/set-tone
- Headspace's 2024 rebrand kept its characters but added photography for "a more professional persona". https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/italic-studio-headspace-graphic-design-project-250424
- The rule for Tualang: speaks at welcome, empty states, step completions and the term recap; silent on errors, constraint failures, data provenance and pricing.

## 6. Live public demo sites

- Plausible's demo is its own live traffic, no login (https://plausible.io/plausible.io). Grafana Play disabled anonymous dashboard creation for security on 15 January 2025. Omeka's sandbox resets four times a week.
- Safety: a demo-mode flag that disables destructive operations and caps sizes; Vercel WAF rate-limit rules need a follow-up deny or 429 (https://vercel.com/docs/vercel-firewall/vercel-waf/rate-limiting); Stripe keeps a test-data banner on screen (https://docs.stripe.com/test-mode).
- QR: width about one tenth of the viewing distance, 6 to 10 cm on an A1 poster, a quiet zone of four modules, the typed URL printed under it, tested on iPhone and Android (**V**: https://www.qr-code-generator.com/blog/qr-code-distance/).

## Ranked wow moments for Lyceum

1. **The live receipt**: the judge clicks a quotation and the employer's posting opens with the sentence highlighted. Honest because the link is real and must survive checking; small.
2. **Tualang's two-minute phone tour**: the judge scans the poster and Tualang walks 9 to 12 steps through the workspace, labelled synthetic on every screen. Honest because the banner never leaves; medium.
3. **The before and after outline slider**: a Juxtapose-style handle between last term's outline and this term's, each change linked to its decision record. Honest because every diff has a recorded reason; medium.
4. **The one-click pack**: a PPTX downloads and opens in PowerPoint, editable. Honest because it is the real generator's output; small, built.
5. **The same question, two doors**: Lyceum's Report beside Claude Desktop answering through the read-only MCP server. Honest because both read one exported file the judge can open; medium.
6. **Count-up to the claims register**: postings, quotations and decisions count up once, with the register beside them. Honest because every figure is already audited; small.
7. **A printed receipt**: a thermal printer prints the quotation, URL, date and a QR code while the judge watches. Honest because it prints a stored record; medium, needs hardware.
8. **The term recap page**: one A4 per semester in a Wrapped style, printed for the judge to keep. Honest because each number traces to a record; medium.
9. **The programme wall**: small multiples of every course in a programme, each a mini loop ring. Honest because it is the same data at another zoom; medium.
10. **A kiosk attract loop**: between judge teams the laptop plays the 60-second real capture and resets on touch. Honest because the capture is of the real app; small.
11. **The named weak spot**: Tualang holds a card listing what is not built, read aloud. Honest by definition, and judges reward it; small.
12. **Decision replay**: scrub a timeline to watch one decision form from quotation to outline change on real timestamps. Honest because it animates stored events only; large.
13. **The judge as lecturer**: the judge names a skill and Watch finds a real posting live, with the recorded fallback one key away. Honest because the fallback is labelled recorded; medium.
14. **A second screen**: a monitor on a stand mirrors the laptop so three judges see without bending. Small.
15. **The peak-end close**: pitch and booth both end on the receipt opening, then a silent bow from Tualang and no new claim. Small.
