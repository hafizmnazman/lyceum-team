# Research note: what a complete course record holds, how attainment is reported, and what the student surveys look like

Date: 22 October 2026, evening. For the depth plan: the sample course must read as a real course record under outcome-based education in Malaysia and internationally, the Learn step must compute attainment the way quality offices do, and the surveys must be real instruments. Compiled in a research session on 22 October 2026. Key: **P** primary (an official standard, template, syllabus or policy page); **S** secondary (a paper or guidance quoting a standard); **V** vendor. Extracted texts of the PDFs cited were saved to the session scratchpad for quoting.

## 1. What a complete course record contains

- **EAC Malaysia course files (the clearest Malaysian list).** "Course files, for every course offered by the programme, provide the course information to include the targeted course learning outcomes, a matrix linking course outcomes to programme outcomes, course synopsis/syllabus, and a list of references. Examination papers complete with answer scheme and graded examination papers with low, medium and high grades, projects, quizzes, tutorial questions, assignments, class projects, copies of the course notes. Sample of projects with low, medium and high grades. Assessment rubrics." Engineering Programme Accreditation Standard 2020, effective 1 May 2020 (UTHM mirror): https://fkaab.uthm.edu.my/docs/EAC-Standard-2020.pdf. The 2024 Standard, effective 1 January 2025, repeats the list (item viii, Appendix C): https://office.iium.edu.my/kca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Engineering-Programme-Accreditation-Standard-2024.pdf **P**
- **MQA course assessment plan columns.** Guidelines to Good Practices: Assessment of Student Learning, 2nd edition, November 2023, Table 1: CLO, CLO statement, taxonomy domain, PLO, topics and content, contact hours, teaching and learning method, assessment methods, weightage; a Table of Specifications (2.5); Appendix 12 "Course Assessment Plan, Instruction and Rubric"; Figure 20 "Overview of the CQI Process". https://ppa.upsi.edu.my/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/DOKUMEN-GGP-AOSL-9223-FINAL-V3.pdf **P**
- **MQA curriculum map down to lessons.** Guidelines to Good Practices: Programme Design and Delivery, 2nd edition, 2023: PLOs map to CLOs and then to lesson outcomes; "each of the CLOs should be connected directly and explicitly to assessment methods and tasks"; Table 2.3 is a PLO-to-courses map for a Bachelor of Computer Science example with enabling, intermediate and culminating indicators; Table 2.4 a programme assessment plan. https://ppq.umk.edu.my/download/SURAT%20MAKLUMAN%20MQA%20BIL.%208%202023_.pdf **P**
- **A Malaysian Course Information document, 2026.** UTM training deck, 2 March 2026: seven elements: a synopsis of 100 to 150 words; CLOs with one measurable verb each, tagged Bloom C1 to C6, P1 to P7, A1 to A5; the CLO to PLO to MQF-cluster map; teaching and learning methods; assessment (continuous 50 to 70%, summative 30 to 50%); student learning time (one credit is 40 hours; a 3-credit example: 14 weeks of 3 hours of lecture, 42; 14 weeks of 1 hour of tutorial, 14; 40 hours of self-study; 12 hours of assessment preparation; 12 hours of final assessment; 120 in all); two or three references under five years old in APA. https://dvcai.utm.my/utmcaq/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/2026/03/29.-Garis-Panduan-Penyediaan-Course-Information_CERAMAH-CI-FC-2.3.2026.pdf **P**
- **MQF 2nd edition, 2024.** Five clusters: Knowledge and Understanding; Cognitive Skills; Functional Work Skills (practical, interpersonal and communication, digital, numeracy, leadership); Personal and Entrepreneurial Skills; Ethics and Professionalism. One credit is 40 notional learning hours. https://office.iium.edu.my/kca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/MQF-V2-2024.pdf **P**
- **A filled course OBE report (in Malay).** UTHM "Laporan OBE Kursus" template, 31 August 2021: CLO to PLO to learning-domain map; CLOs tagged (C4, P4, A4); CLO to teaching method, assessment method and KPI; OBE achievement from the TCIS system; grade analysis overall and per section; CLO attained or not attained with percentages; the CQI report attached. https://fptp.uthm.edu.my/images/FORM_MENU/Quality%20%20OBE/Template%20Laporan%20OBE%20Kursus%20BPB%20-%20Pindaan%20V2%202021.pdf **P** (an example, not a national template)
- **UTP itself.** The Undergraduate Student Handbook Part B (January 2026, updated August 2026) lists TFB3133 Data Visualisation (3 credits, in the Minor in Data Science): JavaScript charts, interactivity, treemaps, heat maps, network graphs, word clouds, timelines, geographic data, D3.js, a mini project; and BBB3103 Data Visualisation (business). No UTP course-outline template, CLO list or OBE handbook is public. https://www.utp.edu.my/sites/Publishing/PublishingContent/StudentHandbook/UG%20STUDENT%20HANDBOOK%20B%20-%202026%20UPDATED%20AUG%202026.pdf **P**
- **ABET.** The EAC Self-Study Questionnaire 2025 to 2026 (E002): Appendix A course syllabi, "maximum length is two pages per syllabus", with a suggested format; Criterion 4 reports assessment processes, frequency, "expected level of attainment for each of the student outcomes", result summaries and how they are documented. https://abet.org/e002_eac-self-study-questionnaire_2025-2026. The CAC criteria 2025 to 2026 give the five computing outcomes plus the computer science outcome 6. https://www.abet.org/accreditation/accreditation-criteria/criteria-for-accrediting-computing-programs-2025-2026/ **P**
- **AUN-QA version 4.0 (August 2020).** A course specification holds the title, prerequisites and credits, expected outcomes (knowledge, skills, attitude), teaching, learning and assessment methods, the outline, assessment details, and "the date on which the course specification was written or revised"; the programme specification needs "a matrix that shows how the programme learning outcomes are achieved through the courses"; requirement 4.4 asks for rubrics, marking schemes and timelines. https://natres.psu.ac.th/qa/doc/CUPT%20QA/aun2020.pdf **P** (mirror)
- **UK.** Lancaster's curriculum and assessment mapping guidance (2017, revised November 2024): programme outcomes limited to 8 to 10, modules marked Introduced, Developed or Assessed, a separate assessment map of methods and weightings. https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/media/lancaster-university/content-assets/documents/student-based-services/asq/pdda/curriculum-and-assessment-mapping/CurriculumandAssessmentMappingguidance.pdf **S**

## 2. How CLO attainment is computed and reported

- **The Malaysian KPI, direct measurement.** UTHM FKEE "OBE Practices 2024": "At least 50% of students achieve 55 marks in all CLO"; "Each PLO achieve at least 60% in average attainment"; the PLO direct measure is a CLO-to-PLO analysis "on all faculty's courses using the average approach (every semester)"; the indirect measure is a yearly graduate exit survey (a five-point Likert mean per PLO) and an employer survey; PEOs by tracer study three to five years after graduation. The OBE system's steps: define PEO, PLO and CLO; key in marks; analyse; "suggest mid/end course CQI"; report by cohort and programme. https://fkee.uthm.edu.my/images/2024/obe2024/2024%20OBE%20FKEE%20Practices.pdf **P**
- The UTHM template above applies the same rule ("50% of students reach 55% (C+) and above") and reports CLO1 84.44%, CLO2 97.04%, CLO3 98.52%. **P**
- **MQA worked targets.** GGP AOSL 2023: indirect evidence is "programme entrance and exit surveys, student interviews, alumni surveys"; example targets "60% of the students score 70 or more in a programme exit examination" and "80% of the students who indicated a score of 4 or higher on the Likert scale in a programme exit survey". **P**
- **A fully specified international formula.** UAE University "Course LOA Process": per assessment tool, attainment is the percentage of students scoring at least 70% of the tool's maximum; the CLO score is the weighted average across tools by the instructor's weights; across sections, weighted by enrolment; a projected minimum of 70%; the weighted score is actual over projected; a course effectiveness score aggregates them; an annual course assessment report. https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/vc/oie/pdf/clo-assessment-process.pdf **P**
- **Weighted CLO to PLO roll-up.** Derouich, "Ensuring Outcome-Based Curriculum Coherence", accepted 13 October 2025 (Discover Education; arXiv 2510.25905): question-to-CLO and CLO-to-PLO weight matrices, PLO as the weighted sum of CLOs, intended against delivered emphasis. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.25905 **S**
- **Direct and indirect disagree.** Hamid et al. (UKM), Pertanika 24, 2016: direct measures (assignment, final exam, presentation, lab report) against a pre and post survey validated with Rasch; "indirect measurement alone is not a valid measure". https://ptsldigital.ukm.my/handle/123456789/577948 **S**
- **A dashboard example.** CLOAP (BRASS Digital Lab, 1 March 2025): an R Shiny tool; the typical threshold "70% of students achieving at least 60% on each CLO", configurable; PLOs aggregated from CLOs. https://brassbe1982-bdlweb.share.connect.posit.cloud/portfolio/cloap.html **V**
- **ABET and AACSB.** ABET Criterion 4 (2025 to 2026): "regularly use appropriate, documented processes for assessing and evaluating the extent to which the student outcomes are being attained". AACSB Standard 5.1 (page updated 9 September 2025): assurance of learning "processes that include direct and indirect measures"; no numeric benchmark set. https://www.aacsb.edu/educators/accreditation/accreditation-process/ai-use-cases-for-accreditation/standard-5 **P**
- Threshold practice varies (55 marks and 50% of students at UTHM; 60 marks and 70% of students in the CLOAP default; 70% of the tool maximum at UAEU), so a product must make the threshold and the KPI configurable per institution and print them beside every attainment figure.

## 3. Student survey instruments

- **SSRT at UTP.** A UTP job advertisement (about four years old) lists "monitoring of T&L quality (e.g. Course Outcome Survey, Student Self Reflection Tools Survey (SSRT), Lecturer's Reflection)" and the use of ULearn. So SSRT is the Student Self Reflection Tools Survey, paired with a Course Outcome Survey (the per-outcome self-rating). No public instrument, scale or schedule was found. https://ng.linkedin.com/posts/universiti-teknologi-petronas_jobvacancy-utpinme-activity-6902713869836394496-fwOL **P** (thin)
- **UTP practice in 2012.** Awang, Singh and Dzulkarnain (UTP), 2012: "students' surveys are conducted at mid-semester and end of semester before the mid semester test and final examination"; ratings 1 to 7, below 5 "unsatisfactory, resulting in the lecturer being tasked to attend" training; 53 courses; a 39% correlation with class grade. https://research.utm.my/wp-content/uploads/sites/80/2013/03/1569534245.pdf **P** (may be outdated)
- **The Malaysian model instrument.** UiTM's SuFO (Rahmat et al. 2025): run each semester for every course, anonymous, 7 professionalism items plus 11 teaching and learning items on a four-point scale (strongly disagree 1 to strongly agree 4), open "two weeks prior to the study week until the formal release of the examination results". https://hrmars.com/papers_submitted/26652/lecturer-professionalism-teaching-and-learning-activities-accounting-students-perceptions.pdf **S**
- **CLO self-rating forms.** UTM's end-of-course survey rates "ability in meeting the course learning outcomes and program learning outcomes on a scale of 1 to 5" (a Scribd copy, unverified); a UPSI study used a five-point self-rating through its LMS (snippet, unverified). The GGP AOSL target "a score of 4 or higher" fits both.
- **SEEQ (Marsh).** A university-hosted form (2018): 29 items in Learning, Enthusiasm, Organization, Group Interaction, Individual Rapport, Breadth, Examinations and Assignments on a 1 to 5 scale, three overall items from Very poor to Very good, plus difficulty, workload, pace, hours a week, prior interest and expected grade. https://www.mckendree.edu/academics/vcte/resources/studentevaluationofeducationalquality.pdf **S**
- **Response norms.** Ahmad 2018 (PSU Research Review 2(3)): Nulty (2008) recommends about 58% for classes under 20 and 35% over 50; Nair et al. 2008 (Monash): 31% online against 56% on paper. https://www.emerald.com/prr/article/2/3/206/325045/Teaching-evaluation-and-student-response-rate **S**
- **Timing, anonymity and release.** Rutgers SIRS: run at the end of each semester, open "during the last week of class meetings", anonymous, "professors will not see the survey results until after they submit final grades". https://sirs.rutgers.edu/ **P**
- **Tooling and export.** Microsoft Forms "Open results in Excel" (a live link or an offline copy): https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/check-and-share-your-form-results-02859424-341d-406f-b32a-9a0fbaf357af. Google Forms "Link to Sheets" and "Download responses (.csv)": https://support.google.com/docs/answer/139706. Moodle's Feedback activity is "ideal for course or teacher evaluations", with an analysis tab and spreadsheet export (docs 5.3, 25 February 2026): https://docs.moodle.org/en/Feedback_activity. Explorance Blue (vendor): https://explorance.com/products/blue/ **P/V**

## 5. Programme-level artefacts judges recognise

- **The courses-to-outcomes matrix.** EAC 2020 self-assessment reports must include "a matrix linking courses to POs to identify and track the contribution of each course" (a sample in Appendix G); MQA GGP PDD 2023 Table 2.3 (enabling, intermediate and culminating ticks) and Table 2.4; AUN-QA requires the same matrix; Lancaster uses Introduced, Developed and Assessed. **P** (Lancaster **S**)
- **The heatmap.** No standard mandates a heatmap; the convention is H, M and L or colour-coded levels (the Anurag CO-PO matrix; a Rajahmundry workshop report, 23 October 2025, teaching "levels of attainment (Low/Medium/High)" matrices for NAAC). Vendor reports: HelioCampus' programme course outcome report (March 2023), a PLO-to-course and CLO mapping with export. **S/V**
- **The CQI loop figure.** UTHM FKEE's flow chart: per course CLO formulation, learning and teaching activities, the lecture plan, delivery and assessment, CLO analysis, CQI at the CLO level feeding PLO analysis, then PEO analysis three to five years after graduation, with stakeholder feedback; cadence: a PLO achievement report each semester, yearly surveys and CQI, external examiner and industry advisory panel reports every one to three years. MQA GGP AOSL Figure 20 and the GGP MR-CIIQ Plan-Do-Check-Act stages with module monitoring from "summative and formative assessments, as well as through student feedback": https://office.iium.edu.my/kca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/GGP-MR_CIIQ-BI.pdf **P**
- **Self-study structures.** ABET E002: background, Criteria 1 to 8, programme criteria, appendices A (syllabi), B (vitae), C (equipment), D (institutional summary). EAC 2020: the report by sections 7.3.1 to 7.3.9 with an Appendix C checklist and Appendix G tables 6 to 16. AUN-QA: eight criteria. The MQA document index at IIUM lists every current MQA title with dates: https://office.iium.edu.my/kca/index.php/mqa-regulatory-documents/ **P**

## What this means for Lyceum

1. The sample course needs the EAC course-file set to read as real: synopsis, five or six CLOs with Bloom tags and MQF clusters, the CLO to PLO matrix, the assessment plan with weights per CLO and a table of specifications, a 14-week schedule with student learning time that adds to 120 hours, references, lecture notes per week, lab sheets, a project brief, a rubric, exam papers with marking schemes, graded samples at low, medium and high, and the per-term OBE report with its CQI note.
2. Attainment must be computed the Malaysian way by default (the share of students at or above a mark threshold per CLO, against a KPI share) with the threshold and KPI as institution settings, the UAEU weighted variant available, direct and indirect kept apart, and the CLO to PLO roll-up as a weighted average.
3. The two surveys are the Course Outcome Survey (one item per CLO, a 1 to 5 scale, target "4 or higher") and the Student Self Reflection Tools Survey (a teaching and learning instrument adapted from SEEQ's dimensions, labelled adapted), run at the end of the semester, anonymous, with results released to the lecturer only after grades, and with response-rate norms shown beside n of N.
4. The content of the sample course is adapted from published syllabi (GWU DATS 6401 first) and says so on the course record; nothing is attributed to UTP's own course.
5. Programme-level views judges recognise: the courses-to-PLO matrix with levels, the CQI loop, and the one-page course OBE report.
